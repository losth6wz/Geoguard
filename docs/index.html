An analyst handoff

Download current evidence to preserve the selected location, quantity, dates and follow-up note. Review candidates before arranging calibrated gas-specific observations matched to satellite time and footprint. An unmeasured point remains unknown.

GeoGuard’s contribution is a shared evidence workflow: quantity switching keeps units, dates and coverage together; records retain their locations; exports support review. Copernicus supplies the measurements and UNEP IMEO supplies the pretrained detector. Reduced review time and improved decisions are proposed benefits that have not yet been measured with users.

Validation plan and current evidence

The notebook reproduces archived atmospheric inputs and replays the dated methane example. Software checks establish reproducibility, not independent detection accuracy. Reviewed positive/negative methane cases and false-alarm performance are not yet established.

Proposed pilot: one willing environmental monitoring partner, one agreed study area and four weeks after access is arranged. Assemble independently reviewed plume and no-plume cases, including cloud/surface confounders. Freeze thresholds before held-out evaluation; report event counts, precision/recall and unknown cases where labels support them. Sparse observations may require a longer collection period.

Seek feedback from at least three intended users on the complete review/export task, recording completion, time and interpretation errors against their existing workflow. No completed interviews, partner commitment or measured benefit is claimed. Expansion requires agreed accuracy/false-alarm targets and evidence that users understand units, dates and unknown results.

Methods, sources and limits

The NO₂, CO, SO₂ and regional CH₄ workflows use Sentinel-5P Level 3 data with the catalog’s quality mask. Granules are averaged within each UTC day before area reduction. Monthly and period summaries weight usable days equally. No conversion to ground-level concentration, AQI or source attribution is performed.

The grid spacing of approximately 1.1 km does not mean individual streets can be resolved. Methane screening uses native 20 m shortwave-infrared measurements on a 10 m processing grid, score > 0.5 and at least 100 connected pixels. Scores are not methane density, emission rates or calibrated probabilities. The public-image adapter’s UAE accuracy is not independently validated. Bundled NO₂ evidence contains area values and history without a raster overlay; CO, SO₂ and regional CH₄ include archived map layers.

Example observation windows: NO₂: January–August 2026. CO, SO₂ and regional CH₄: August–September 2026. SO₂ uses an assumed ground-level vertical profile; this does not make it a surface concentration. Regional CH₄ uses OFFL and albedo-bias correction; sparse coverage and residual retrieval artifacts remain. PM mass, VOCs and H₂S require unconnected instruments/data. Aerosol index is not PM mass.
